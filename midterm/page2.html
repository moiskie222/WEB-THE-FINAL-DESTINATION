Our story

Good pizza brings people together.

PizzaHouse began with a simple idea: make honest, delicious food and make everyone feel at home. We stretch our dough fresh, prepare our sauce in-house, and bring the same care to every pizza we serve.

What makes our dough special? +

We prepare it fresh and give it time to rest, so every crust bakes up light, crisp, and full of flavor.

Can you accommodate dietary needs? +

Our team is happy to talk through ingredients and available options. Please contact us before ordering.
